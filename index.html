Our Products

RMC

RMC is a specialized material in which cement, aggregate, and other ingredients are weight batched at a center batching plant, mixed either at the plant itself or in truck mixers, and then transported to the construction.
The technique is useful in congested sites or at diverse workplaces and saves the consumer from the botheration of procurement, storage, and handling of concrete materials.
